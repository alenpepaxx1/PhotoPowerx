## 🌟 Overview

**PhotoPower Studio** is a cutting-edge, professional web-based photo editing and AI image transformation studio engineered with high-performance canvas shaders, GPU-accelerated algorithms, and real-time interactive studio lighting. Designed for photographers, designers, and content creators, PhotoPower delivers desktop-grade editing capabilities directly in the browser.

---

## ✨ Key Features & AI Modules

### 💡 1. 3D Virtual Studio Relighting Engine (`3D Light`)
* **Multi-Light Source Support:** Add and position unlimited 3D virtual lights (*Sunlight*, *Spotlight*, *Cyberpunk Neon Rim*, *Softbox Beauty*, *Rembrandt Focus*).
* **Precise 3D Placement & Controls:** Manipulate Light Position (X, Y, Z depth), Color Kelvin/Hex, Intensity, Falloff Radius, and Ambient Fill.
* **Studio Presets:** Instantly apply studio lighting rigs like *Cyberpunk Neon*, *Golden Hour Sunset*, and *Hollywood Rembrandt*.

### 📷 2. AI DSLR Depth Bokeh & Lens Blur Studio (`Bokeh`)
* **Aperture F-Stop Presets:** Ultra-realistic lens depth-of-field simulation ranging from **f/1.2** (Ultra Bokeh), **f/1.4**, **f/1.8** (Classic 50mm Prime), **f/2.8**, **f/5.6**, to **f/22** (Deep Landscape).
* **Interactive Focus Targeting:** Click or drag focus coordinates (X, Y) and adjust the sharp subject focal range.
* **Aperture Blade Shapes:** Choose aperture specular highlights shapes including *Circular*, *6-Blade Hexagon*, *5-Blade Pentagon*, and *Anamorphic Oval*.
* **Highlight Bloom:** Boost specular highlights in out-of-focus background regions.

### 🪄 3. AI Magic Eraser & PatchMatch Inpainting (`AI Eraser`)
* **Content-Aware Removal:** Effortlessly erase unwanted objects, blemishes, or text using PatchMatch AI texture synthesis.
* **Inpainting Modes:**
  * *PatchMatch AI:* Fills regions seamlessly using surrounding texture patterns.
  * *Boundary Blend:* Smooth gradient patching ideal for skin retouching.
  * *Grain Matching:* Preserves authentic film grain texture over edited areas.
* **Mask Controls:** Adjustable brush size, feathering softness, and smart edge contour detection.

### ☁️ 4. AI Sky & Atmosphere Replacement (`AI Sky`)
* **1-Click Atmosphere Presets:** Zëvendësoni qiellin me *Golden Hour Sunset*, *Deep Milky Way Galaxy*, *Northern Aurora*, *Fluffy Blue Sky*, or *Dramatic Thunderstorm*.
* **Horizon Edge Matting:** Precise control over horizon height position, feathering blend width, opacity, and ambient lighting color matching.

### 🔤 5. Typography & Text Inspector Engine
* **25+ Google Fonts:** Pre-loaded luxury serif, modern sans-serif, poster display, calligraphic script, developer monospace, and arcade fonts (*Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, Playfair Display, Bodoni Moda, Cinzel, Oswald, Bebas Neue, Orbitron, Fira Code, Press Start 2P, UnifrakturMaguntia*).
* **1-Click Studio Typography Presets:**
  * ⚡ *Cyber Neon:* Cyan glow with magenta stroke.
  * 👑 *Vogue Editorial:* High-contrast tracked luxury serif.
  * 🔥 *Poster Impact:* Bold yellow display headline with drop shadow.
  * 👾 *8-Bit Arcade:* Pixel art gaming text style.

### 🛡️ 6. Watermark & Anti-Theft Security Grid
* **Anchor Positioning:** Place custom text/logo stamps on a 9-point grid layout.
* **Full-Screen Tiled Security Pattern:** Automatically project a diagonal repeating watermark pattern across the full image canvas to prevent unauthorized copying.

### ⚡ 7. 1-Click AI Smart Auto Enhance
* Intelligent 1-click enhancement algorithm that analyzes image metrics and automatically balances Exposure, Shadows, Highlights, Sharpness, Vibrance, and Color Temperature.

## 🚀 How to Use PhotoPower Studio

1. **Upload an Image:** Drag & drop any photo onto the main canvas or click **Upload Image**.
2. **Left Toolbar & Top Navigation:** Access main workflow modes (*Adjust*, *FX*, *Layers*, *Text*, *Crop*).
3. **Right Sidebar Studio Panels:**
   * Open **3D Light** to drop virtual 3D spotlights onto your subject.
   * Open **Bokeh** to select aperture presets like `f/1.2` and target focal points.
   * Open **AI Eraser** to brush over and remove unwanted background items.
   * Open **AI Sky** to swap sky backgrounds with dramatic sunsets or galaxies.
   * Open **Text** to add custom typography with custom Google Fonts and neons.
4. **History & Undo/Redo:** Use the bottom timeline bar to undo/redo changes or compare before/after states.
5. **Export:** Click **Export** to save your masterpiece in high-resolution JPG, PNG, or WebP.
